The framework

What is IPM?

Every activity is measured using three independent dimensions. No single score can fully describe athleticism.

I — Intensity

Physical effort, cardiovascular demand and exertion required to compete.

P — Precision

How accurately the body must control movement, timing and coordination.

M — Movement

The range, frequency and amount of bodily movement involved.

IPM scores describe physical demands, not whether a game deserves respect. Starter estimates are not scientific measurements. Community ratings are opinions and may change as more people participate.
